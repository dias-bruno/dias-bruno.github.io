Research

My doctoral research, Developing standards for equity in transportation planning and policy, combines accessibility analysis, survey microdata and statistical modelling to ask how much access to opportunities is considered sufficient, and for whom. Earlier, my work used remote sensing and machine learning to map urban and socio-environmental patterns in Brazilian Amazonian cities and precarious settlements in São Paulo, Brazil.
